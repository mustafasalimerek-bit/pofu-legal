Privacy Policy

Pofu · Erek Studio

Short version: Pofu has no accounts and no sign-in, and we collect nothing about you — your progress stays on your device. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google processes device data to serve them. Pofu Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple; with it the game shows no ads.

What we collect

We — Erek Studio — do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no sign-up, no profile, no account, and no analytics service in the app. The one exception is the advertising, where Google processes data we never see or store; see “Advertising” below.

What is stored on your device

Pofu saves a few values locally so it can remember where you are:

  • Which level you have reached, and your best result (stars and touches) on the levels you finished.
  • Your daily puzzle results and streak, the pofu you have discovered, your achievements and the skin you chose.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, colour-blind marks, the daily reminder, and the language you picked.
  • Whether you own Pofu Pro, so the game knows without asking the store every time.
  • How many levels you have won since the last full-screen ad, and when it was shown, so ads are paced; and when the game last asked for a rating, so it does not ask again too soon.

This never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app. “Reset progress” in Settings clears your progress but not your purchase.

Purchases

Pofu Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your name, card or Apple ID. The app only learns whether Pro is owned, and “Restore purchases” in Settings asks the App Store again.

Advertising

The free game is supported by ads: a full-screen ad between levels every so often, never while you are playing a board, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a help — another blast, your last touch back, clearing one hazard, a hint, more touches after you run out, escaping a black hole, or keeping a daily streak. None of them is ever required to finish a level. With Pofu Pro there are no ads at all: the full-screen ads stop and every help is given instantly, without a video.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google processes your advertising identifier, an approximate region derived from your IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad, under Google's privacy policy. We only ever see aggregate revenue reporting — never anything about an individual person. Pofu does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK), a Google consent form asks for your choice before ads are served, and Privacy options in the game’s Settings lets you change it at any time.

Notifications

After you finish your first daily puzzle, Pofu asks whether you would like a daily reminder. If you say yes and allow notifications, the reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off in Settings or in your device’s settings.

Children

Pofu is rated 4+ and has no chat and no user content. The only links out of the game are this policy, the terms, the support page and the purchase screen in Settings, and the ads described above. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Playing offline

Every level and every daily puzzle is stored in the app, so the game is fully playable with no network. Ads cannot load offline, so a help you ask for is simply given without a video.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date below.
